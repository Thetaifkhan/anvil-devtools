UUID v4 vs v1

v4 UUIDs are generated from random bits and are the most common choice today. v1 UUIDs encode a timestamp and a node identifier; since browsers don't expose a real MAC address, this generator simulates the node portion with random bits, which is standard practice for client-side v1 generation.

FAQ

Are these UUIDs cryptographically secure?

v4 UUIDs here are generated with crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographically secure random source.
