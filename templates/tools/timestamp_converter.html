What is a Unix timestamp?

A Unix timestamp counts the seconds (or milliseconds) elapsed since January 1, 1970, 00:00:00 UTC — the "Unix epoch." It's timezone-independent, which is why it's the standard way to store and exchange dates across systems.

FAQ

My timestamp looks 1000x too large — why?

You likely have a millisecond timestamp but selected "Seconds," or vice versa. JavaScript's Date object uses milliseconds internally, while many APIs use seconds — switch the unit toggle and try again.
